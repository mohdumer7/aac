<product_requirements>
The goal is to develop a comprehensive HRMS application for Acero Building Systems to automate HR processes and replace manual forms. Key requirements include:
1.  **Core HR Forms**: Digitization and management of 9 specific HR forms (e.g., Manpower Requisition, Candidate Information, Employee Information) with drafting capabilities and all specified fields.
2.  **Flexible Approval Flows**: A new, visual flow design system enabling dynamic approver assignments, conditional logic, parallel/sequential steps, and audit trails.
3.  **PDF Generation**: Auto-generation of filled forms (PDFs) based on captured data, including approval history.
4.  **Workflow Continuity**: Seamless progression of data and forms through a continuous workflow (e.g., Manpower Requisition leading to Candidate Information), with pre-filled and disabled fields for previous steps, and free navigation between steps.
5.  **Master Data Integration**: Utilization of existing master models (users, departments, organizations, locations, countries) for data consistency and dropdown selections.
6.  **Employee ID Generation**: Automatic generation of unique employee IDs if not provided.
7.  **Draft Management**: Ability to save and view draft workflows and forms, ensuring no data loss, with proper debouncing for auto-saves.
</product_requirements>
